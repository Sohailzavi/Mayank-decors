4 · The calculator

This is the main part. You enter only the width and height, and the app works out cloth, lining, stitching, track, tie belts and installation using your rules, the same way every time. Please try it with real sizes and tell us if any number is different from how you calculate.

Rules from you, already in the app
  • All sizes are converted to inches
  • Curtain: 1 panel per 20″ width, cloth per panel = (height + 15″) ÷ 39 m
  • Wide 110″+ fabric: 1.5 m per panel, only under 110″ height
  • Roman: 52″ usable width, (height + 15″) ÷ 39 × panels, lining same
  • Roller / zebra: (height + 6″) × width ÷ 144
  • Roman and roller: minimum 15 sq ft
  • Wallpaper: 50 sq ft per roll, always a full roll
  • Murals: fixed price per size, trimmed on site
